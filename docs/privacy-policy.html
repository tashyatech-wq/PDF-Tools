Privacy Policy for PDF Tools

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Tashya Labs Pvt Ltd ("we", "us") operates the PDF Tools mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

1. PDF processing

All PDF operations performed by the App (merge, split, compress, unlock, and scan-to-PDF) happen locally on your device. The files you select are not uploaded to our servers or any third party to perform these operations.

2. Camera

The App requests camera access only for the Scan tool, which lets you capture a paper document and convert it to a PDF. Camera access is used solely for this purpose and is requested only when you use that tool.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google AdMob may collect and use the advertising identifier and related diagnostic data to serve and measure ads. You can review how Google handles this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Data we do not collect

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect your name, email address, or the contents of your files on our own servers.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to tashya.tech@gmail.com.
